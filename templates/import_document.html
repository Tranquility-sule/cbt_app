Import Questions from Word or PDF

Upload a .docx or .pdf file containing objective questions, and it'll be scanned for questions in this format: 

1. What is the capital of Nigeria?
A. Lagos
B. Abuja
C. Kano
D. Ibadan
Answer: B

Numbering can use "1." or "1)". Options A-E can use "A." or "A)". The "Answer:" line is optional — for Word documents, a bolded option is also recognized as the correct answer if no Answer line is given. You'll get a chance to review and fix every detected answer before anything is saved.
